Log Analyzer to Ergo replay

Open Log Analyzer in a new tab

Sign in normally in a new tab first. The embedded view is optional: SSO, third-party cookie settings or the website's frame policy may block it. If it is blank or refuses to connect, use the new-tab link. This viewer cannot bypass those restrictions or read the embedded page.

  1. In Log Analyzer, open the VIN and time window from NUC storage, or upload your existing local raw capture files.
  2. Check the SDB and Interfaces, then plot the required seat, steering and mirror signals.
  3. Choose Export data..., select plotted signals and a continuous time range, choose MDF4 (.mf4), then Export. Download the generated file.

Opening a log in the Analyzer does not download its raw captures to this PC. Raw captures, indexes and export jobs stay on its server. Ergo uses only the MF4 you explicitly download; it cannot extract the Analyzer's server cache or browser memory. A local PCAP/BLF must first be uploaded and decoded in the Analyzer.

Save completed .mf4 files into data\logs\ relative to this project. Use your browser's Save as dialog or move the download there. This page cannot set the browser download folder or detect closure of the external website.

When finished, close this dialog to convert new or changed files and load the first replay. Independent files stay separate. Conversion requires the local ergo3d serve server, not an offline HTML or Teams preview.

Log Analyzer exports decoded samples, not plot-reduced points. Ergo resamples them at 10 Hz with linear interpolation; this does not prove that the recording is gap-free or its clocks are synchronized. Export physical position/angle channels from a known continuous test interval, not discrete commands or status text.
